# BETWEEN TWO SUNS — External Benchmark Learnings (DO NOT COPY)

These brands are reference points for commercial/creative standards, not visual templates. We must create an ownable BTS system.

## Rhode
Use as a benchmark for:
- the routine/kit as a first-class purchasable object
- clear original value vs kit price
- reviews close to purchase
- "what's inside" and "the routine" clarity
- complete-your-routine logic
- highly sensorial product/texture media
Do NOT copy:
- grey minimalism
- glazed/dewy vocabulary
- Rhode's layout, typography, photography, product shapes, bag/kit treatment

## KraveBeauty
Use as a benchmark for:
- direct product naming + immediate benefit descriptor
- kits / bundles as clear commerce
- review richness by skin type / concern
- "Why is it different / Helps with / Made for" explanatory structure
- strong skincare education without becoming a textbook
Do NOT copy:
- barrier-first brand language
- their graphic style, colors, naming tone, icon system

## Topicals
Use as a benchmark for:
- Gen Z energy and strong color ownership
- direct add-to-bag behavior
- shopping by concern
- bundles and routine value
- personality / culture without losing product clarity
Do NOT copy:
- their specific pink/red/green palette
- loud patterning or existing campaign styling
- their slogan voice

## Beauty In Stem
Use as a benchmark for:
- editorial minimalism with immersive motion
- science-led storytelling
- strong system/routine framing
- 2026 award-recognized UI/UX/innovation
Do NOT copy:
- "skin fitness" concept
- their typography / 1/2/3/4 visual language
- their exact motion language

## Hims / Hers
Use as a benchmark for:
- frictionless commercial hierarchy
- concern-first navigation
- "how it works" clarity
- ingredient education written for normal people
- direct buy/learn-more separation
- trust and service architecture
Do NOT copy:
- telehealth / clinical tone
- medicalized UI
- their product architecture

## BTS synthesis
BTS must feel:
- more art-directed than Hims/Hers
- more commercially explicit than Beauty In Stem
- more ownable/less trend-dependent than Rhode
- cleaner/more premium than Topicals
- more visually contemporary and sensorial than KraveBeauty

The synthesis should NOT look like a collage of these brands.

Ownable BTS assets:
- exact tall condensed BETWEEN TWO SUNS wordmark
- exact )( "between" icon
- 4 matte pack colors: mint / powder blue / lilac / peach
- 4-step routine architecture
- AM = 4, PM = 3
- climate-adapted product truth, used sparingly
- "fresh skin. always."
- product formulation education that explains WHY ingredients/formula architecture matter
