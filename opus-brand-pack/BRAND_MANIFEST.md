### Color rule
Pantone U values above are the print source of truth. Do not treat the current v7 CSS hex values as brand standards. Digital colors should be color-managed approximations derived from the Pantone targets and final photographed packs. The palette must stay soft, sophisticated, matte and product-led — never candy-saturated or glossy.

### Material rule
Physical packaging is matte / soft-touch. Digital product light should preserve that finish. Avoid plastic shine unless present in the real closure / cap.

## Typography
- The tall condensed BETWEEN TWO SUNS wordmark is a fixed brand asset and should be used exactly from the SVG.
- Condensed display typography is part of the visual language, but it must be rationed. Do not turn every section into a huge stacked poster headline.
- Functional ecommerce typography should be exceptionally legible, neutral and modern.
- Arabic / RTL is a first-class art-direction requirement. Do not merely mirror English. Pair a strong Arabic display face with a highly legible Arabic UI face and redesign line breaks / hierarchy intentionally.

## Non-negotiable creative correction
Skincare must be the hero. "Climate-adapted" should be felt through formulation logic, light, real-life use, proof and one or two brand moments. It should NOT dominate every headline / interaction / screen.

Recommended content balance (directional):
- 30% desire / brand world / personality
- 25% product / texture / sensoriality
- 20% efficacy / ingredients / proof / trust
- 15% routine / education / AOV
- 10% climate-adapted differentiation
