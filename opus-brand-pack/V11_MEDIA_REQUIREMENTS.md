# BETWEEN TWO SUNS — V11 MEDIA REQUIREMENTS
Lead: Claude Opus

Do not generate assets randomly. Generate/produce only against this list and only after the design slot is fixed.

## MUST-HAVE — Pack Accuracy / Hero
### H1 — Individual hero pack surface, 4 assets
One per SKU.
- Ratio: 4:5 master + 9:16 crop.
- Subject: exact final product pack, upright.
- Surface: same family colour as physical pack.
- Lighting: clean directional daylight/studio hybrid, matte pack, crisp real contact shadow.
- Composition: enough negative space for cropped wordmark/UI.
- No transparent cutout look.
- Exact label and closure must be preserved.
- Preferred production: photography / high-fidelity product composite.
- AI: acceptable only if exact pack is composited from verified artwork/render afterward.
- Purpose: hero Swatch Rail / shop cards / paid social.

### H2 — Four-product family hero
- Ratio: 16:9 + 4:5 + 1:1 crops.
- All 4 packs, exact relative scale.
- One coherent lighting environment.
- Clean, graphic, tactile; no random props.
- Purpose: routine/bundle, social, email.

### P1 — PDP pack suite, each SKU
- Ratios: 1:1.
- Front, 3/4, back, closure/cap detail.
- Neutral or matching surface.
- Exact final pack text and geometry.
- Purpose: PDP gallery / quick view.
- Photography/high-fidelity composite preferred.

### P2 — Pack in hand, each SKU
- Ratio: 4:5.
- Casual, believable hand grip; clean nails; no jewellery overload.
- Range of skin tones across set.
- Purpose: scale/tactility, PDP and social.
- Photography preferred; AI acceptable for concept/internal prototype if exact pack is composited afterward.

## MUST-HAVE — Texture
### T1 — Texture macro loop, 4 assets
- Ratio: 9:16 + 1:1 exports.
- 4–6 seconds.
- Reset: fresh translucent gel/lather behavior.
- Clarity: lightweight serum droplet/spread.
- Barrier: light cream peak/spread.
- Defense: lightweight SPF slip/spread/absorption.
- Lighting: crisp macro, tactile, editorial, no cliché water splash.
- Background: controlled near-neutral or SKU family tone.
- Purpose: Drag-to-Swatch + texture wall.
- AI acceptable for prototype if physical behavior is plausible; real product photography preferred for launch.

### T2 — Texture hero still, 4 assets
- Ratio: 1:1 and 4:5.
- Derived from T1 visual language.
- Purpose: reduced-motion fallback, PDP, cards, email.
- AI acceptable.

## MUST-HAVE — Application / Skin
### A1 — Product application, 4 vertical clips
- Ratio: 9:16.
- 6–10 seconds each.
- One product per clip.
- Real face/hand application.
- Range of Egyptian/GCC-relevant skin tones across series.
- Natural skin texture; avoid over-retouch.
- Purpose: PDP How to Use / social.
- Photography/video preferred; AI acceptable for internal prototype, not as efficacy proof.

### S1 — Real skin close-up library
- Ratio: 4:5.
- At least 4 skin tones.
- Real pores/texture.
- No before/after claim implied.
- Purpose: brand world, texture context, PDP.
- Photography preferred.

### S2 — SPF finish / white-cast demonstration
- Ratio: 4:5 + 9:16.
- Multiple skin tones.
- Apply actual amount, show finish.
- Purpose: substantiate "no white cast" visually.
- Real product photography/video strongly preferred for public launch.
- AI not acceptable as proof; can be used as layout placeholder only.

## ROUTINE
### R1 — AM layering sequence
- Ratio: 9:16.
- ~30s.
- Reset → Clarity → Barrier → Defense.
- Purpose: routine page / educational social.
- Photography/video preferred.

### R2 — PM layering sequence
- Ratio: 9:16.
- ~20–25s.
- Reset → Clarity → Barrier.
- Purpose: routine page.
- Photography/video preferred.

### R3 — Routine tabletop still
- Ratio: 4:5 + 16:9.
- All four products on common surface.
- Purpose: bundle module / CRM.
- Photography or high-fidelity composite.

## COMMUNITY / CULTURE
### U1 — UGC-style vertical clips, 6–8
- Ratio: 9:16.
- Locations: bathroom mirror, vanity, handbag, desk, post-gym, car-mirror SPF reapply where safe/not driving.
- Natural social framing, not polished ad framing.
- Product visible but not always centered.
- Purpose: community strip / paid social / email.
- AI acceptable for internal prototype. Real creator UGC preferred at launch.

### U2 — Bag/desk/vanity stills, 4–6
- Ratio: 4:5.
- Editorial but casual.
- Avoid trend-cliché props.
- Purpose: lifestyle/culture breaks.
- AI acceptable if product is accurately composited.

## BRAND / ABOUT
### B1 — Founder/brand portrait
- Ratio: 4:5.
- Only if real founder story is used.
- Purpose: About.
- Real photography only.

### B2 — Formulation/lab
- Ratio: 3:2 / 4:5.
- Real lab/team/materials only if available.
- No fake scientist imagery.
- Purpose: About/trust.
- Real photography only.

## REVIEW / SOCIAL PROOF MEDIA
### RV1 — Sample review photo slots
- Internal prototype only.
- 1:1 casual skin/product images.
- Must be visibly marked SAMPLE/PLACEHOLDER if fabricated.
- Never labelled verified.
- Replace before production.

## CRO / SERVICE SUPPORT MEDIA
### C1 — Payment/COD/delivery
No decorative photography required. Use clean text/UI, real provider marks only after gateway choices are finalized.

## Priority order
P0: clean pack assets / alpha-fringe fix or accurate composites
P1: H1 + T1/T2
P2: P1 + A1 + S1
P3: S2 + R1/R2/R3
P4: U1/U2
P5: real About imagery

## Rules
- No AI skin image may be presented as product-result proof.
- No AI review person may be presented as a real reviewer.
- No final product image may contain invented label text or geometry.
- Exact pack artwork is composited after generation whenever AI cannot preserve typography.
- The design should accept both mobile and desktop crops without changing the story.
